Where & how I work

Studios & rig

Studios across Alberta

I don’t lock you into one room. I record in partner studios across Alberta and pick the one that suits the song: a big live room for drums that need air, a vocal booth for a quiet take, or a rehearsal space when the band sounds best playing together.

You pay the studio’s room rate, and I book it and set it up. Mixing happens in my own room.

DAWs & plugins

I work in Pro Tools, Logic Pro and Reaper, so I can open your session in whatever you recorded it in, and sessions move between studios without surprises.

Mixes run through UAD, Waves, Soundtoys and FabFilter plugins: classic console, tape and vintage compressor emulations for character, FabFilter for clean, precise EQ and dynamics, and Soundtoys for saturation, delays and the weird stuff.

  • Pro Tools
  • Logic Pro
  • Reaper
  • UAD
  • Waves
  • Soundtoys
  • FabFilter
