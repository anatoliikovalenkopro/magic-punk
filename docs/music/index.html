Where & how I work

Studios & rig

Studios across Alberta

I don’t lock you into one room. I work with partner studios across Alberta and help you pick the one that suits the song: a big live room for drums that need air, a vocal booth for a quiet take, or a rehearsal space when the band sounds best playing together.

You pay the studio’s room rate, and I book it and set it up. Mixing happens in my own room.

The mixing chain

Every session runs in Pro Tools, the studio standard, so sessions move between rooms without surprises. Mixes go through UAD plugins: emulations of classic consoles, tape machines and vintage compressors, the tools behind the records you grew up on.

  • Pro Tools
  • UAD plugins
  • Console & tape emulation
  • Vintage compressors & EQs
  • Reference monitoring
